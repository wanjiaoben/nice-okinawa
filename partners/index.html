05 / QUESTIONS

Questions

I have no sales experience. Can I still apply?

Yes. Tell us which fields and languages you know and how you reach people, and we'll find a suitable project and way to work together. Whether we partner, and on what terms, is agreed by both sides.

How do I register the customers I introduce?

We'll agree on the registration method before we start. Always get the customer's consent first, then submit the required details as agreed. For now, please contact us through our usual channels.

What if the same customer is introduced by more than one partner?

Customer ownership, registration validity, existing customers and duplicate introductions are explained and agreed before we start. Please don't assume ownership based on forwarding records alone.

Can I promote more than one project?

Yes, you can express interest in several. Products, customer registration and commission terms are confirmed separately for each project.

Do I need to quote prices, collect payment or make promises on your behalf?

No. Your role is to introduce customers. Quotes, contracts, payment and service are handled by our company — just pass the customer's needs to us.

Is there a fixed salary?

No. This is an independent referral partnership with no fixed salary; commissions are paid when the agreed conditions are met. Specific terms follow what both sides confirm before we start.
