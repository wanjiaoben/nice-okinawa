What to Do If a Typhoon Affects Your Trip to Okinawa

Typhoons are a normal part of life in Okinawa, especially during summer and early autumn. Most visitors remain safe by checking reliable information and avoiding unnecessary travel.

Before the Typhoon

  • Check the latest warnings from the Japan Meteorological Agency.
  • Confirm the status of your flight, ferry, bus, rental car and booked activities.
  • Charge your phone and prepare drinking water, food and any necessary medication.
  • Follow instructions from your hotel or accommodation.

During the Typhoon

Stay indoors and keep away from windows, beaches, ports and the sea. Waves and wind can become dangerous very quickly.

Even if the weather suddenly becomes calm, do not go outside until the official warning has been lifted. Transportation may also be suspended, so avoid unnecessary driving.

If Your Plans Are Affected

Flights, ferries and outdoor activities may be delayed or cancelled. Contact your airline, accommodation or tour operator directly for the latest information.

You can also check the Official Okinawa Travel Guide for current safety advice.
