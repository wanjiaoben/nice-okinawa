Travel agencies: your land operator and local suppliers in Japan

We are the local desk in Japan for overseas travel agencies and tour operators, arranging transport, accommodation, activities and other local suppliers to fit your itinerary.

Who you are. You sell Japan itineraries abroad, or you are about to add Japan or Okinawa to your programme, and you need a partner who gets things done on the ground.

What you need in Japan. One desk that takes care of the local arrangements: transport, accommodation, activities, and the confirmations and adjustments before and after departure.

How we help. Send us your itinerary or requirements. We propose the local arrangements, deal with each local operator, complete the bookings and keep following up before and after your guests arrive. You deal with one desk only.

Language is not a barrier. Many local operators worth putting in an itinerary don't speak English. We deal with them for you throughout, arrange an on-site interpreter if needed, and follow up from booking until the trip is over.

Our registration, 旅行サービス手配業 (Travel Service Arrangement Business), is the registration under Japan's Travel Agency Act for arranging transport, accommodation and other travel services within Japan on behalf of travel companies.

CATALINA JAPAN K.K. is a company based in Okinawa, Japan, trading as nice.okinawa and registered with the Governor of Okinawa Prefecture as a Travel Service Arrangement Business (沖縄県知事登録 旅行サービス手配業 第143号). That is Japan's registration for land operators: arranging transport, accommodation and other travel services within Japan on behalf of travel companies.

Car rental, chauffeur-driven cars (ハイヤー) and fishing boats are provided by locally licensed operators. We have worked in Okinawa for more than ten years and have a wide local network: from choosing the right operator to booking, changes and follow-up on the ground, one desk handles it all, so things run smoothly.

We can follow up anywhere in Japan. Our main focus is Okinawa, and we also have staff in Tokyo, Osaka and Hokkaido.
