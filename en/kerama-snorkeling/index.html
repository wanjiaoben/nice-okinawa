Who can join

  • Please tell us each guest's age when you enquire. We'll check with the operator what's suitable for your family.
  • Non-swimmers welcome: life vests worn at all times, staff keep watch
  • Not available if pregnant, or with conditions requiring daily medication (high blood pressure, diabetes); health waiver signed on site
  • No alcohol the night before — smelling of alcohol means no boarding, no refund
  • Fun divers: send cert card photo, last dive date/place, and logged dives

Bring: swimwear (rash guard recommended), towel, drinks, motion-sickness pills. Approximate prescription masks available; contacts work with a normal mask.

Cancellation policy

Safety comes first. The captain makes the final decision on the day. If the captain cancels because of unsafe weather or sea conditions, there's no cancellation fee.

20 days or more before the tour: free; 19–7 days before: 50%; 6–2 days before: 80%; the day before, on the day, or no-show: 100%.

More boarding rules (read before you go)
  • No smoking or alcohol on board; take rubbish back with you
  • No mineral sunscreen paste — use waterproof reef-friendlier SPF50 lotion
  • Seasick-prone? Take a pill before departure
  • Leave jewellery ashore (seawater corrodes, earrings snag); lost/damaged rental gear charged at cost
  • Board shorts over briefs-style swimwear, please; rash guards beat bikinis for sunburn
  • Boat space is tight — leave luggage and valuables in your car or hotel

FAQ

Where is Chibishi?

Chibishi is a group of small islands in the Kerama area, about 30 minutes by boat from the Okinawa main island, known for its clear emerald-green water.

Where do we meet?

The meeting and return point is Ginowan Marina. We'll send the exact meeting time and a map link once the captain confirms your tour. Hotel transfers are not included, but we can check options for you on request.

Is it a morning or afternoon tour?

Both. The morning tour usually starts around 8:00 AM and the afternoon tour around 1:00 PM, and each lasts about 4 hours. Exact times depend on the day, so we'll confirm them when the captain confirms your tour.

Is this a private boat?

No. This is a shared boat tour, not a private charter, so other guests may join. If you'd like the whole boat for your group, we can check a separate private charter quote.

Will we definitely go to Chibishi?

Not always. The captain chooses a safe snorkeling spot based on the day's weather and sea conditions. If Chibishi isn't safe that day, we'll go to another suitable spot.

How much does it cost?

JPY 21,000 per person for the shared boat tour. Optional one-on-one staff support for guests who aren't confident swimmers is an extra JPY 11,000. Private charters are quoted separately; just ask.

What's included?

English-speaking Japanese staff; snorkeling equipment and life jacket; basic beginner fishing gear and bait; underwater photos and videos; boat insurance; drone footage, weather permitting.

I'm not a strong swimmer. Can I join?

Yes. Everyone wears a life jacket, and English-speaking staff are on board. If you're nervous, you can add one-on-one staff support during snorkeling for JPY 11,000. Please tell us your swimming level when you book, and always follow the captain's and staff's instructions.

Can children join?

Please tell us each guest's age when you enquire. We'll check with the operator what's suitable for your family.

What if the weather is bad?

How do I book and pay?

Once we confirm availability, we'll send a link for a temporary card pre-authorization. This is a hold, not a charge. You pay in cash on the tour day, and we release the hold after the tour. Your booking is confirmed only after availability and the pre-authorization have both been verified.

What's the cancellation policy?

20 days or more before the tour: free; 19–7 days before: 50%; 6–2 days before: 80%; the day before, on the day, or no-show: 100%.

What should I bring?

Wear your swimsuit under your clothes, and bring a towel, dry clothes, food, drinks and non-slip shoes. If you get seasick, prepare motion-sickness medicine in advance and follow its directions.

Are there showers after the tour?

Yes. Hot showers are available at Ginowan Marina for JPY 300 per person (optional, paid on site).

What information do you need from me?

Full name, age, nationality (for the passenger list and insurance), height, weight and shoe size (for equipment), a phone or WhatsApp number that works in Japan, your hotel, your swimming level, and any relevant health concerns.
