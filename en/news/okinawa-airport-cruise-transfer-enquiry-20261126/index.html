Arriving in Okinawa by air or cruise ship? What to include in a transfer enquiry

2026-11-26 · Nice Okinawa

Okinawa Prefecture’s preliminary October figures, released on 25 November, recorded 195,300 foreign arrivals by air, up 7.5% year on year, and 47,200 by sea, down 40.4%. The sea total includes 14,200 crew members and others; it is not a count of visitors who went sightseeing ashore.

For an airport pickup, give your date, flight number, party size and destination. For a cruise call, include the ship’s name, port, date, party size and all-aboard time. Landing or docking time is not necessarily when you will be ready to meet your driver.
