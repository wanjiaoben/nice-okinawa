Okinawa boat trips on 24 November: check your departure before heading to the harbour

2026-11-24 · Nice Okinawa

A forecast published on the morning of 24 November by the Naha Coast Guard Office called for northeasterly winds of 40 knots and four-metre waves in the waters south of Okinawa by 3 p.m. local time. Thunderstorm, strong wind and high wave advisories were also in effect that morning for the Kerama Islands and the Onna area.

Four metres is a forecast for an offshore area, not a reading at your snorkelling site or a notice that all boats are cancelled. If you have booked snorkelling, a fishing trip or a private charter, ask your operator whether your trip is running, whether the departure port has changed, and exactly when and where to meet before leaving for the harbour.
