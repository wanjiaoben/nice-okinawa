Naha Opens Shurijo Castle Reconstruction Support Grant: Up to ¥100,000 for Solo Projects and ¥300,000 for Joint Projects

2026-11-23 · CATALINA JAPAN

Naha City opened applications on 2 November 2026 for its FY2026 Shurijo Castle Reconstruction Support Grant. Applications close on 29 January 2027. Eligible applicants include small and medium-sized businesses with a head office, branch or other business base in Naha; sole proprietors based in the city; and groups whose representative or main office is located in Naha.

The proposed project must first be approved by Okinawa Prefecture as an official Shurijo Seiden Completion Commemorative Support Project. Eligible expenses may include promotion directly related to the completion of the reconstructed Seiden, development of new products or services, and necessary project administration. In principle, costs must arise after the grant decision and be ordered, contracted and paid within the eligible period.

The subsidy rate is up to one-half of eligible expenses. The ceiling is ¥100,000 for a project conducted by one applicant and ¥300,000 for a joint project by multiple businesses. The same project may not receive overlapping support from the national government, Okinawa Prefecture or another public funding body. The ceiling is not ¥4.85 million, and prefectural recognition as a Support Project is not the same as approval for the Naha City grant.

This article provides information only. It does not state that our organization is applying for, has received, or represents applicants for this grant.

Naha-based businesses planning a Shurijo-themed product, service or promotion should first confirm prefectural Support Project approval, then check eligibility and the grant-decision date before incurring expenses.
