Japan records its busiest October for arrivals from Taiwan: where to meet your ride at Naha Airport

2026-11-24 · Nice Okinawa

The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) estimated on 18 November that Japan welcomed 634,600 arrivals from Taiwan in October 2026, up 6.5% year on year and a record for October. The figure is for Japan as a whole, not for Okinawa.

Before flying into Naha, check whether you booked an airport transfer or a shuttle to a rental car office. For a transfer, ask which exit to use, what sign to look for and whom to contact if your flight is delayed. For a rental car, the shuttle stop may not be where you collect the car. Follow the instructions from the office you booked with.

For a quote, include your flight number, passenger count and number of large bags. Mention pushchairs or long pieces of equipment so that the operator can check luggage space.
