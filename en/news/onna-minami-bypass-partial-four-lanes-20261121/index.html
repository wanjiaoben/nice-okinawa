Partial four-lane operation planned for Onna Minami Bypass on November 21

2026-11-20 · Nice Okinawa

In its November 13 announcement, the Okinawa General Bureau said partial four-lane operation is planned to begin at 10:00 JST on November 21, 2026, within the approximately 2.7-kilometre section of National Route 58’s Onna Minami Bypass between Tancha and Nakadomari Intersection. The changeover time may be adjusted due to weather or other conditions.

This is not the opening of four lanes along the entire bypass: traffic restrictions will remain in some areas. Drivers should follow signs and traffic controllers, allow extra time for hotel check-in, activity meeting times and transfers, and not rely solely on navigation estimates.
