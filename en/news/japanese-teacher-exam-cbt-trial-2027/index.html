Japan plans a February 2027 CBT trial for the Japanese Language Teacher Examination; full transition remains undecided

2026-11-10 | Shinko Lab

MEXT plans a computer-based testing (CBT) trial for the Japanese Language Teacher Examination on February 6 and 7, 2027, across all 47 prefectures. It will be free, with limited capacity and a lottery if oversubscribed. It is separate from the formal examination and will not determine pass or fail.

A trial is not confirmation of a full transition

MEXT says a move to CBT from fiscal 2027 onward is under consideration. This does not confirm that the 2027 formal examination will be fully computer-based. Online applications are planned to begin after December 18, the scheduled date of the 2026 results; exact arrangements remain subject to further announcements.

What prospective participants should check

FAQ, application guidance and a page illustrating the experience are planned for November. Reading the news early does not guarantee a place. Bookmark the official page, distinguish trial registration from formal-exam registration, and plan around official updates. Taking part in the trial cannot replace passing the formal examination or obtaining the qualification.

Official source: 文部科学省
