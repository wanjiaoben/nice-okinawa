Dismantled vehicles and used parts: what to check before quoting on a shipment from Japan

2026-11-23 · CATALINA JAPAN K.K.

A November 9 warning published by Japan’s Ministry of the Environment concerns an Ibaraki exporter that declared a UAE-bound shipment to Yokohama Customs as dismantled vehicles and used auto parts. An inspection and subsequent investigation found that the load included items classified as waste under Japanese law. The exporter had not completed the required waste-export procedure and received a formal warning. This was an individual case, not a UAE ban on used cars from Japan.

When sourcing dismantled vehicles or mixed loads of parts, look beyond the description on the invoice. Before quoting, ask the supplier for a packing list and photos of the actual goods, and check their origin and condition. If you cannot establish whether anything is classified as waste, consult the relevant authority before issuing a quote.
