Returning a rental car in Tomigusuku on November 25? Check this stretch of Route 331

2026-11-25 · Nice Okinawa

Bridge work is scheduled to close the section of Route 331 from the Municipal Road 191 intersection to the Nakachi (名嘉地) intersection in the direction of Tomigusuku City Hall, from 9 p.m. on November 25 to 6 a.m. on November 26, Japan time. The closure affects one direction only. It is not a closure of Naha Airport.

If your rental return or airport transfer takes you through this section, check the official traffic map before setting out and confirm the route and meeting time with your rental office or driver. The night of November 26 is a reserve work period, not a confirmed second closure.
