Mori employers can claim part of the cost of Japanese lessons after staff pass the JLPT

2026-11-28 · Shinko Lab

Mori Town in Shizuoka Prefecture updated its guidance on 17 November. Employers with a workplace in the town who hire foreign staff can run classes or enrol staff at a school or class serving non-native speakers of Japanese. Online lessons qualify too. The aim must be to pass JLPT N3 or higher; training confined to job-specific terminology or operating manuals is excluded.

Employers pay in full first. They may claim eligible expenses for staff who pass N1, N2 or N3 in the fiscal year and are still employed when the application is filed. Instructor fees, tuition, teaching materials and the relevant examination fee may count. The grant covers up to half of eligible costs, capped at ¥200,000 per employer per fiscal year. Applications for this fiscal year close on 15 March 2027. Grants are paid within the town's budget. Keep proof of the result, employment and payment, together with the course details.
