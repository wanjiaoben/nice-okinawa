Taking the JLPT in Japan on December 6? Bring a paper test voucher

2026-11-23 · Shinko Lab

Japan Educational Exchanges and Services (JEES) has mailed vouchers for the December 6 Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) in Japan. If yours had not arrived by November 20, or you have lost it, sign in to MyJLPT, print the replacement voucher and bring it to the test. A screenshot on your phone is not accepted.

Check the building name on your voucher and plan your route. N1 and N2 start at 9:10 a.m.; N3, N4 and N5 start at 3:10 p.m. Those are test start times, so plan to arrive earlier.
