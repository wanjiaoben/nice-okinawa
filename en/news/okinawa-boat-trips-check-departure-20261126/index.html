Okinawa boat trips on 26 November: check your departure before leaving

2026-11-26 · Nice Okinawa

At 09:20 Japan time on 26 November, the Naha Coast Guard Office listed a thunderstorm advisory with a risk of sudden gusts for areas including central and southern Okinawa Island, Onna and Kin, and the Kerama Islands. Its 06:15 forecast for waters east of Okinawa called for easterly winds of 20 knots strengthening to 30 knots from the northeast by 15:00, with three-metre waves.

This is a regional forecast, not a reading at an individual snorkelling spot or a blanket cancellation notice. Before heading to your meeting point for a fishing trip, charter or snorkelling tour, give the operator your port and departure time and confirm whether your trip is going ahead.
