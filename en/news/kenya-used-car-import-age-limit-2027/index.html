Shipping a used car from Japan to Kenya? A December departure is not enough for a 2019 registration

2026-11-28 · CATALINA JAPAN

In a notice dated 24 November, the Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) said that from 1 January 2027 only right-hand-drive used vehicles first registered on or after 1 January 2020 will be admitted. The date to check is first registration, not manufacture.

Vehicles imported from Japan must carry a Certificate of Roadworthiness (CoR) issued by QISJ on behalf of KEBS. Vehicles first registered in 2019 will not be admitted after 31 December 2026, when their certificates also expire. Sailing from Japan before year-end does not extend that deadline. Before bidding or paying a deposit, ask the exporter to check the registration date, certificate and expected arrival for the individual vehicle. If arrival slips into January, consider a right-hand-drive vehicle first registered in 2020 or later.

Source: KEBS / MyGov, 24 November 2026 (p. 9)
