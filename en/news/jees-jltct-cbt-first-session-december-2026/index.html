Which Japanese teaching test starts on 1 December?

2026-11-26 · Shinko Lab

On 20 November, JEES released a computer-based testing guide for its Japanese Language Teaching Competency Test. The first sitting is on 1 December 2026. This is not the examination for Japan’s national Registered Japanese Language Teacher qualification.

Counting back under the official business-day rules gives 26 November for credit-card bookings and 25 November for convenience-store or Pay-easy bookings for that first sitting only. Watch the guide on the JEES website, then check availability at your preferred test centre.
