Moving an Okinawa boat trip to Sunday, 29 November? Check the sailing, not just the date

2026-11-28 · Nice Okinawa

Moving a Saturday fishing charter or snorkelling trip to Sunday morning does not guarantee calmer seas. In its forecast issued at 07:00 Japan time on 28 November, the Okinawa Meteorological Observatory forecast north-easterly winds of 35 knots and four-metre waves in waters south of Okinawa on Sunday. Winds were forecast to ease to 30 knots and waves to three metres by 21:00 on Sunday. A near-gale warning is in effect for these waters.

This is a regional forecast, not a measurement at your port or a cancellation notice. Give the operator your departure port, route and sailing time, and ask when a final decision will be made. If the trip is unconfirmed, keep your booking flexible or plan a land-based alternative. Check the latest forecast and operator notice before leaving.
