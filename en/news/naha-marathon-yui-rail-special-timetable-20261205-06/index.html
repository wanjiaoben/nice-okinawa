Heading to Naha Airport on marathon weekend? Check the December 5–6 monorail timetable

2026-11-23 · Nice Okinawa

Okinawa’s Yui Rail announced on November 10 that trains will run on special timetables on December 5 and 6 for the 40th NAHA Marathon. On race day, December 6, the operator expects crowded trains from the first service until about 9 a.m. as runners travel to the event. This is a crowding advisory, not a service suspension.

If you have a morning flight, are returning a rental car before taking the train, or need to reach an exam venue, work back from the time you need to arrive. Use the operator’s notice to check the date, boarding station and direction, and allow time for the car return, the walk to the station and the wait on the platform.
