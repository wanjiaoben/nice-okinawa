Getting around Okinawa: what to check before choosing a ride

At airports and ports, check how to board and who operates the ride. Plate colour alone does not establish legality.

What was announced

On October 30, 2026, the Okinawa General Bureau reported an awareness campaign against unlicensed taxi and bus operations. Activities took place at Naha Airport on October 1, Naha Port and Shimojishima Airport on October 5, and Ishigaki Port on October 6. This was a report on earlier activities, not a new rule or an operation on October 30.

Finding a ride on arrival

Use Naha Airport’s official taxi-stand guidance. Do not follow a driver merely because they approach you. MLIT says chauffeur-car services require advance booking; airport solicitation is not permitted.

Booking ahead

As a practical travel precaution, ask for the operating company, contact details, meeting point and fare conditions. Keep the booking record. This checklist does not certify any provider. If the operator is unclear, pause before confirming.

Do not judge by plate colour alone

The relevant question is whether paid transport has the required permission or registration. Japan has authorised arrangements using private vehicles, so a white plate alone does not prove illegality. This does not establish availability of any particular service in Okinawa.

Check again before departure

Consult current airport and operator information because arrangements may change. This article does not arrange vehicles, endorse a company or guarantee fares or availability.
