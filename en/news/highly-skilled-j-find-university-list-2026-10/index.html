Japan Updates October 2026 University Lists for Highly Skilled Professional Points and J-Find

November 5, 2026 | Catalina Japan Co., Ltd.

On November 4, 2026, Japan’s Immigration Services Agency updated both the university list used for Highly Skilled Professional points and the list of universities eligible under the J-Find program, based on rankings as of October 2026.

  • Under the Highly Skilled Professional points system, graduating from a university designated by the Minister of Justice is a 10-point category. The updated list is used to check that category.
  • J-Find is a separate program. Applicants must hold a degree from an eligible university ranked in the global top 100 in at least two of the three specified rankings, apply within five years of receiving the degree, and satisfy all other requirements.
  • University-based points, J-Find university eligibility and Japanese-language points are assessed separately. In the HSP table, JLPT N1-equivalent ability (for example BJT 480 or above) may correspond to 15 points, while N2-equivalent ability (for example BJT 400 or above) may correspond to 10 points, subject to restrictions on duplicate points.
  • Listing does not guarantee a residence status. Applicants should verify the latest three rankings, degree and graduation date, planned activities, income, other eligibility rules and required evidence for their own case.

Do not confuse university eligibility with a Japanese-language score

Graduating from a listed university does not automatically secure a visa, and a BJT score alone does not make someone eligible for J-Find. Catalina Japan provides Japanese study and BJT preparation, not immigration eligibility decisions or application representation.
