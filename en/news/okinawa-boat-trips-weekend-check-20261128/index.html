Okinawa boat trips on 28 November: waves forecast to reach 4 metres by Saturday evening

2026-11-27 · Nice Okinawa

If you have booked a fishing charter, private boat trip or boat-based snorkelling tour, check your departure port, meeting time and route with the operator before heading to the harbour.

In its 07:00 forecast on 27 November, the Okinawa Meteorological Observatory said waves in the southern East China Sea and in waters east and south of Okinawa were expected to reach 4 metres by 21:00 on 28 November. Northeasterly winds south of Okinawa were forecast to reach 35 knots. All times are local. This is a regional marine forecast, not a wave reading for every port or a blanket cancellation notice. If your sailing changes, ask the operator about rescheduling or an alternative itinerary.

Source: Okinawa Meteorological Observatory marine forecast, carried by the Japan Coast Guard
