TOURISM MARKET UPDATE · 2026.11.03

Okinawa Recorded 5.33 Million Visitors in the First Half of FY2026 as Taiwan Air Capacity Expands

November 3, 2026 | Nice Okinawa / Catalina Japan Co., Ltd.

Okinawa Prefecture’s October 26 flash report recorded 5,333,800 visitors for April–September 2026. The total was lower than the same period a year earlier, while a new Naha–Kaohsiung service and additional Naha–Taipei capacity create practical opportunities for winter travel products serving Taiwan.

5,333,800first-half visitors (flash)
-3.6%year on year
1,433,700international visitors (flash)

What this means for travel products

  • Both total and international visitor numbers were below the prior year, so the data should not be described as a full recovery.
  • New Kaohsiung service and more Taipei capacity can support airport transfers, Naha-based marine activities and short-stay island itineraries.
  • Winter priorities include whale watching, small-group charters and weather-resilient backup plans, with Traditional Chinese guidance for Taiwan and Hong Kong guests.

These figures are preliminary. Okinawa Prefecture plans to publish final first-half figures in December 2026, and the totals may be revised.
