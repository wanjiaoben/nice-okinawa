Shurijo Castle Seiden Opens at Noon on 23 November: Timed Entry Required, Adult Admission Rises to ¥1,000

2026-11-23 · CATALINA JAPAN

Shurijo Castle Park has announced that the completion ceremony for the reconstructed Seiden will be held on 22 November 2026, followed by public opening on 23 November. Because an opening ceremony is scheduled that morning, general access to the Seiden begins at 12:00 noon on the first day.

Individual visitors who wish to enter the Seiden must book an admission ticket with a 30-minute timed reservation. There is no additional fee for the reservation itself. Annual-pass holders and visitors eligible for free admission must also reserve a time slot to enter the Seiden. If spaces remain on the day, tickets may be purchased online or at the Kōfukumon ticket office up to 15 minutes before the time slot. Visitors who only want to see the Seiden exterior and other paid areas may use a ticket without internal Seiden access.

From 23 November 2026, individual admission is ¥1,000 for adults, ¥760 for high-school students, ¥370 for elementary and junior-high students, and free for children under six. The official site recommends public transportation. Visitors driving to the park should allow extra time for nearby traffic and parking. The estimated visit from Suimuikan, including the Seiden interior, is about 90 to 120 minutes.

For visits from 23 November onward, reserve the Seiden time slot first, then plan rental-car pickup and return, parking and nearby attractions. A general admission ticket alone does not necessarily include entry to the Seiden interior.
