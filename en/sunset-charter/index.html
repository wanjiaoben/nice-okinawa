FAQ

Can I book this as a shared boat?

No. This sunset plan is private charter only and does not accept shared boat bookings. The maximum is 12 guests.

What time does the boat go out?

The usual time is about 17:00-19:00, adjusted by the day's sunset time. The whole trip is about 2 hours.

Can children join?

Yes. Children can join this private sunset charter.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Around 18:00 the evening before, we confirm based on sea conditions. If sea conditions or typhoon mean the boat cannot go out, guests can choose a full refund or free reschedule.

How do I pay?

Please contact us by WhatsApp or email first. The charter starts from ¥60,000, and extra requests are quoted by group size and request.
